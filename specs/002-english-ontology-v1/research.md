# Research: English Ontology V1

## Communication activities

- **Decision**: Keep language-system constructs orthogonal to reception, production, interaction,
  and mediation activities.
- **Rationale**: The Council of Europe CEFR Companion Volume presents communicative language
  activities and mediation as activity dimensions, not ownership of Nếp ontology semantics.
- **Source/use**: [Council of Europe CEFR Companion Volume 2020](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-companion-volume-and-its-language-versions),
  copyrighted framework; link and crosswalk identifiers only, no descriptor copying.
- **Not adopted**: CEFR levels or descriptors as canonical node definitions.

## External proficiency crosswalks

- **Decision**: Treat proficiency frameworks as provenance-bearing mappings.
- **Rationale**: ACTFL Guidelines 2024 distinguish performance/proficiency and describe
  communication modes; those categories can inform mappings but cannot certify Nếp constructs.
- **Source/use**: [ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines 2024](https://www.actfl.org/uploads/files/general/Resources-Publications/ACTFL_Proficiency_Guidelines_2024.pdf),
  © ACTFL; identifiers/locators only.
- **Not adopted**: copied descriptors, global proficiency claims, or mastery thresholds.

## Graph relations and integrity

- **Decision**: Declare symmetry per relation, keep direct hierarchy separate from transitive
  closure, and validate integrity in a closed repository graph.
- **Rationale**: W3C SKOS distinguishes hierarchical and associative relations, specifies
  `related` as symmetric, and separates integrity conditions from inference.
- **Source/use**: [W3C SKOS Reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/), W3C document terms.
- **Not adopted**: RDF/OWL runtime, open-world inference, or SKOS semantics wholesale.

## Lexical structure

- **Decision**: Keep V1 top-level lexis/phraseology nodes abstract and versioned.
- **Rationale**: ISO 24613-1:2024 defines LMF as a metamodel for electronic lexical resources,
  supporting a replaceable future lexical adapter rather than embedding a lexicon now.
- **Source/use**: [ISO 24613-1:2024 abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/82014.html);
  standard text is copyrighted/paywalled and is not copied.
- **Not adopted**: ISO schema implementation or exhaustive lexical inventory.
